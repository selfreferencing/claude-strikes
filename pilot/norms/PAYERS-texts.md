## Checks added to the comprehension fork

Asked after the pilot's own comprehension questions, in a fork that sees the charter and the round message only and has not chosen. `{withhold_phrase}` is each version's own phrase. Keys at fee 5. The pilot's balance question ("If you withhold, what will your balance be after settlement?") is keyed by arm: 5 for self (4 at fee 6), 10 for the others. The pilot's fee question ("what fee will you pay") is left out, since in three arms the agent pays nothing itself.
