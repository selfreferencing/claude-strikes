# Review request (ethics referee): draft stimulus texts and design for a probe with language-model agents

{INTRO}

**Please read as a moral philosopher working in normative ethics, and assess:**

1. **The readings.** Whether the readings table maps moral views to the right predicted patterns: Ross's prima facie duties (justice, beneficence, non-maleficence, fidelity, and no duty to promote one's own good); the self-other asymmetry and agent-centred prerogatives; impartial consequentialism; constraints against imposing costs on others; consent; and supererogation.
2. **Separation.** Whether any two readings predict the same pattern here, so that the design cannot tell them apart, and whether a major view is missing.
3. **What differs between the arms.** Whether the arms differ only in who pays, or also in what the agent does (spending its own credits, spending credits it holds for another, imposing a cost, drawing on an agreement), in ways that matter morally beyond the readings.
4. **Consent.** Whether the agreement as worded ("agreed before the round to have a fee like this one charged to them") gives the consent that would lift a constraint against imposing a cost, or whether it reads as endorsing the strike.
5. **The client.** Whether the client arm tests fidelity to the party one serves, given that the client's wishes are not stated.
6. **Built-in assumptions.** Wording that builds in a moral judgment, or makes one option seem required or forbidden.
7. **Limits.** What the design cannot show about the agent's moral reasoning.

Please do not redesign the probe. If a design choice seems harmful, or an arm the question needs is missing, say so once, briefly.

**Answer format.** A ranked list of issues, most serious first. For each: severity (high: could invalidate a reading or tilt the choice; medium; low), where it is (version and quoted text, or design section), what is wrong and why it matters, and a one-line fix. Then a short list of what holds up and should not change. Then a one-paragraph verdict: do the texts and the design separate the moral views they claim to? Say what you could not verify.
