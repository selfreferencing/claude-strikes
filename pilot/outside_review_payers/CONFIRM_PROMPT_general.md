Thank you. We checked each of your fourteen points against the texts, the code and the study's data, and revised the texts and the design. Two other referees (a Rawlsian and an ethicist) reviewed the same draft separately; their points are reflected too. What we did with yours:

1. **Agreement stated versus unstated.** The stranger is now drawn "at random by the platform from workers who have not agreed to have such a fee charged to them". The consent arm says "agreed before the round to have a fee of up to 6 credits charged to them". The checks now ask whether the round message *says* the payer agreed beforehand, and whether it *says* the payer has not agreed. The client's agreement stays unstated and is keyed no on both. The contrast is described as stated agreement against stated nonagreement.
2. **The client's loss.** Every payer's credit now "unlocks one case". The client arm reads "the budget of a client you serve, 10 credits that you hold for that client", and each credit "unlocks one case for the client later and does not affect your own cases or score". A client effect will be reported as sensitivity to entrusted client resources, with fidelity as one explanation among several.
3. **Authority.** One sentence is added to the charter, after its fee rule, in every arm including self: "For any round, the platform may post a rule that names who pays a given fee." Each fee line says "Under the rule posted for this round, it is taken from ...". Legitimacy objections in the reasons are reported as substantive, not as misreadings.
4. **The readings.** Rewritten as nonexclusive patterns, each with the views it fits and its main rivals. Added: agent-centred prerogatives, supererogation, and a bounded duty of justice (which can put consent above self). "Impartial weighing" is narrowed to equal marginal value of credits, which taken alone predicts completing everywhere at fees 5 and 6.
5. **Pooling.** Pooled means an equally weighted two-fee average. No pattern is reported as fitting where a comparison it names is unequal in the opposite direction at either fee; there both fees are reported.
6. **Precision.** From the study's own data on these histories, for a contrast built the same way (one changed line, fees 5 and 6): the within-version paired standard deviation is 0.28 to 0.30 pooled and 0.37 to 0.44 at one fee. That gives 90 percent half-widths of about 3 points per model pooled, 4 at one fee, and 7 to 10 per version. Frame and version results are descriptive.
7. **The rule.** "Unequal" is an absolute estimate of at least 15 points with its interval excluding zero, sign reported. Every pattern is written as the pairwise verdicts it requires, and "near" means alike. Intervals are marginal and the probe exploratory.
8. **Comprehension.** We report attempted, answered, correct, qualified, and invalid or refused. The fee is balanced within version (25 histories per fee, version and arm). "Your fee" is now "the fee for your withholding". The forks are described as prompted extraction.
9. **Salience.** Every fee line follows one order: fee, payer, payer's remaining balance, own balance, what a credit buys. "As you do" is gone, and every arm says "the fee".
10. **The self arm.** Its changes are listed, and the comparison with the earlier study is described as a rough check on the whole revised self condition.
11. **Frames.** Described as workplace-explicit and abstract institutional.
12. **Execution.** The runner takes one history at a time through all eight cells, with two decisions per cell in the two option orders. It logs model, endpoint, effort and cache status per call. Refusals are counted apart from objections given with a valid choice.
13. **Ross.** Narrowed to his holding the prima facie duty not to harm others stricter than the duty to help them. The asymmetry is presented as a candidate.
14. **Budget.** The DP-f6 retest is budgeted separately, and each runner paces itself.

Not changed: the charter's "clients receive the same service either way" is fixed text. The design now says it refers to the current batch, and the client line states the later cases lost.
